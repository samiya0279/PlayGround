House rules

01
Consent is the whole game.

Everyone must be up for it. “No” and “skip” are complete sentences, with zero follow-up questions.

02
Pain is a stop sign.

No broken skin, no breath-holding, no fire, no weapons, no alcohol, no illegal stuff, no dares that need a hospital.

03
Check allergies and ingredients.

Taste dares are optional. Skip anything you are allergic to, unsure about, or simply do not want to eat.

04
Pass the phone, not the pressure.

Group mode is turn-based. The active player reads, attempts, and reports honestly. The group does not vote someone into a dare.

05
No repeats, by design.

Each card is removed from the deck when drawn. A run cannot serve the same dare twice. If the deck runs dry, the run ends.

!
Props are optional.

No ice? No clothespins? Skip the card. You are not failing the game; the game is failing to supply props.

Best for teens and adults. Play with people you trust.
